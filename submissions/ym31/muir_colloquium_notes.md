# Colloquium Notes (Jessie Muir)

- Cosmology from imaging surveys, mostly DES
- only ~5% of the universe (normal matter) actually has a physics explanation, dark matter and dark energy (~70%) are basically just names
- LCDM = dark energy is a cosmological constant + GR + cold dark matter
- treat dark energy as a fluid with w = P/rho, expand w in the scale factor -> w0, wa. LCDM is w0=-1, wa=0
- timeline of the evolving dark energy thing: DES SN + CMB ~2 sigma (2024), DESI DR1 almost 4 sigma, DR2 ~4.2, now after the SN recalibration everything is more like 3 sigma
- DES (not DESI, confusing names) is imaging so redshifts are photo-z from colors. bad for single galaxies but ok since they only need the distribution. 5000 deg^2, 6 years
- DES BAO + DES SN + CMB is ~2.4 sigma in the same direction as DESI, so kind of an independent check on the BAO part
- Weak lensing changes shapes by <1% so you need tons of galaxies and look at shape correlations vs seperation on the sky
- 3x2pt = shape-shape, position-position, position-shape. galaxy bias enters each one differently so using all 3 helps
- tomographic bins from photo-z. ~11 million lens galaxies, ~140 million source shapes
- main parameters are Omega_m and S8 (how clumpy matter is). they cut small scales because of baryons
- analysis is blinded, her own work is on the blinding and the checks before unblinding
- Y6 S8 vs CMB is ~1-2 sigma, she didnt really call it a tension, and it hasnt changed much from Y1 to Y3 to Y6
- w0wa: 3x2pt alone is weak but lets you drop SN or BAO and still get something. everything combined ~3 sigma from LCDM. evolving DE doesnt fix the S8 thing
- Q&A: someone asked if the error bars could be underestimated, DES uses an analytic covariance that got checked a lot in Y3. She's wait and see on dynamic dark energy since the contours are long and almost parallel, so the combined result can move a lot
